Technology-driven engineering company specializing in subsea innovation.

Subseatronics is a technology-driven engineering company specializing in innovative subsea and industrial solutions at the crossroads of electronics, software, automation, and robotics.

Founded by Lucas Marion, Subseatronics was established to design, integrate, and deploy smart, high-performance systems capable of operating in the most demanding marine and industrial environments.

Originally from Réunion Island and now based in the south of France, Lucas Marion leads the company with a global vision supported by a strong hands-on engineering culture. Subseatronics operates worldwide, working in close collaboration with its clients to transform complex technical challenges into efficient, scalable, and future-ready solutions.

From advanced subsea equipment integration and intelligent sensor interfacing to custom electronics, embedded software, industrial automation, and mechatronic R&D, Subseatronics drives innovation from concept through deployment.

With strong expertise in ROV development—including trenching ROVs, TDM ROVs, mini ROVs, and next-generation underwater machinery—the company delivers agile, reliable, and cutting-edge technologies for high-performance marine operations.
