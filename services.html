INDUSTRIAL

We specialize in the integration of advanced subsea industrial equipment for complex electrical and electronic systems. Our expertise covers the full lifecycle of industrial projects, from system design and equipment selection to installation, testing, and commissioning.

Subseatronics delivers reliable, robust, and scalable solutions tailored to harsh subsea environments. We ensure seamless interoperability between electrical, electronic, and control systems, meeting the highest industry standards for safety, performance, and durability.

Our solutions are designed to optimize operational efficiency while minimizing downtime, making us a trusted partner for demanding subsea industrial applications.

Our approach is based on a deep understanding of subsea operational constraints and industrial performance requirements. We collaborate closely with clients, manufacturers, and classification societies to ensure that every integrated system meets strict safety, reliability, and compliance standards. By focusing on system interoperability, redundancy, and ease of maintenance, we deliver robust industrial solutions designed for long-term offshore operations in the most demanding environments.

SHIP INTEGRATION

We provide complete integration and interfacing of specialized sensors on survey and offshore vessels. Our solutions enable precise positioning, navigation, and bathymetric data acquisition, essential for high-accuracy marine operations.

Our ship integration process includes detailed system architecture design, careful cable routing, power management, and synchronization of all onboard sensors. We perform thorough dockside and offshore testing, calibration, and verification to ensure optimal system performance. By minimizing signal interference and ensuring precise time alignment between systems, we guarantee accurate, reliable, and repeatable data acquisition for survey, inspection, and offshore construction operations.

ELECTRONICS

Subseatronics designs, manufactures, and customizes electronic circuit boards to meet exact project specifications. Our electronics are engineered for reliability, precision, and performance, even in the most challenging environments.

From initial schematic design to final production, our electronics development process is driven by strict quality standards and extensive testing procedures. We select components specifically suited for marine and industrial applications, ensuring resistance to vibration, temperature variations, and harsh operating conditions. Our in-house capabilities allow us to support rapid prototyping, design optimization, and controlled production runs, providing clients with reliable and scalable electronic solutions.

SOFTWARE

We develop custom software solutions tailored to specific operational requirements. Our expertise includes system control, data acquisition, monitoring interfaces, and real-time automation.

Our software solutions are designed to operate reliably in mission-critical environments where stability, cybersecurity, and performance are essential. We develop intuitive user interfaces combined with robust backend architectures, enabling seamless communication between sensors, control systems, and automation hardware. Through careful testing, validation, and long-term support, we ensure that our software remains adaptable, secure, and efficient throughout the entire system lifecycle.

R & D

Our R&D department engineers and delivers turnkey mechatronic solutions for both marine and terrestrial applications. We manage the entire development process, from feasibility studies and design to prototyping, testing, and deployment.

Our multidisciplinary R&D teams combine mechanical engineering, electronics, and software development to create innovative, high-performance systems. We focus on transforming complex concepts into practical, field-ready solutions through iterative design, advanced simulation, and extensive testing. This approach allows us to reduce development risks, optimize performance, and deliver fully validated mechatronic systems tailored to real operational challenges.

ROV SPECIALIST

Subseatronics is a specialist in the design and development of trenching and TDM ROVs, Mini ROVs, and advanced underwater machinery. Our systems are built for high-performance marine operations and demanding subsea tasks.

Each ROV is engineered with a strong emphasis on durability, power efficiency, and operational flexibility. We design modular platforms that allow easy integration of tooling, sensors, and mission-specific payloads. Through rigorous testing and continuous optimization, our ROVs deliver reliable performance, precise control, and high productivity, enabling operators to successfully execute complex subsea intervention, inspection, and construction tasks.
